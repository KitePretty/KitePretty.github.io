My research experience spans patient portals, online health communities, and eating-disorder mutual-aid organizations. Across these settings, I examine how people make sense of health information and give and receive support.

I apply mixed methods to understand these experiences. I enjoy moving from identifying a problem to finding the communities it matters to and working with them to co-design technologies or systems. I see AI as potentially complementing rather than displacing human connection. I remain skeptical that technology can solve every problem and situate my research in the particular contexts and relationships through which care takes place.
