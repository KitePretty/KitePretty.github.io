## Overview

This project examines an accessible bar in Shanghai as an urban third place that expands opportunities for disabled people to participate in nightlife and social life. I explore the psychological and social dimensions of accessibility: feeling welcome, at ease, and accepted in a shared space.

I am particularly interested in how an inclusive environment makes room for disability as part of ordinary human diversity, and how this understanding is sustained through everyday interactions.

## Approach

The project began with one month of participant observation and informal conversations with 20 people. I am currently analyzing the material to understand how accessibility is experienced and sustained in everyday social life.
