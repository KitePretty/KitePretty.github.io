I am a research assistant in the [School of Data and Information Sciences](https://sdis.unc.edu/) at UNC–Chapel Hill, working with Dr. [Franklin Mingzhe Li](https://franklin-li.com/). Previously, I was a predoctoral researcher in Georgia Tech’s [School of Interactive Computing](https://ic.gatech.edu/) (2025–2026), advised by Dr. [Shaowen Bardzell](https://www.ic.gatech.edu/people/shaowen-bardzell) in the [ESCape Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/escapelab/Home).

I study how technology can support healthcare before diagnosis, during treatment, and through recovery, both inside healthcare institutions and in everyday settings beyond them. I am particularly interested in the needs and experiences of marginalized and vulnerable populations. My work spans **accessibility**, **women’s health**, and **queer youth**. I am also exploring AI as a potential way to accompany people through the stretches of care that fall outside clinical settings.

I hold an MA in Global Communication from CUHK’s [School of Journalism and Communication](https://research.cuhk.edu.hk/en/organisations/school-of-journalism-and-communication/) and a BA in Network and New Media from Sichuan University’s College of Literature and Journalism.

I am seeking PhD opportunities for Fall 2027.
